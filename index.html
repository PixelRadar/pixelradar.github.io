PixelRadar

Video game deals, free games and simple tech explainers for Italian gamers.

PixelRadar tracks Steam, Epic Games Store and PlayStation Plus prices every day and turns the best deals into short videos on Instagram and TikTok (@pixelradar.it). A Telegram bot, @PixelRadar_bot, alerts followers when a game drops in price.

PixelRadar Uploader

PixelRadar Uploader is our internal desktop tool. It publishes PixelRadar's own videos, with their captions, to the PixelRadar TikTok account and reads the account's statistics for our internal dashboard. It is not offered to other users.
