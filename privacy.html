PixelRadar Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What data the App accesses

  • Basic profile information of the PixelRadar TikTok account (display name).
  • Account and video statistics (followers, likes, views, comments, shares) of the PixelRadar account.
  • The list of videos published by the PixelRadar account.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok to authorize uploads and posts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish PixelRadar's videos and to show the account's own statistics on an internal dashboard. The App does not access data of other TikTok users.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored only on PixelRadar's own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access stops the App from working; locally stored tokens then become invalid.
